Help

Good questions.

How long until I fall asleep?

It depends on the night. Most sessions are 20 to 45 minutes, and they fade out on their own once you drift off.

Will it keep playing all night?

No. Every session ends with a slow fade and then silence. You can also set a sleep timer.

Can kids use it?

Yes. The Family plan has gentle stories made for little ones, with Pip as their guide.

Is this medical advice?

No. Hushwing helps you relax and wind down. If sleep problems last, talk to a doctor.

Does it work offline?

On Plus and Family, yes. Download tonight's story and keep the phone in airplane mode.
